From **20 January 2027** the Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies. It replaces the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC and applies directly throughout the EU. If you build, modify or link machines, now is the time to review your risk assessment and documentation.

De Machinedokters build special-purpose machines and retrofit existing ones. We handle CE marking ourselves: risk assessment, technical documentation and declaration of conformity.

## What changes with the Machinery Regulation?

- **Substantial modification.** The regulation defines when a rebuild is so far-reaching that whoever makes the change takes on the manufacturer's obligations. This matters if you modernise or modify machines.
- **Digital documentation.** Under certain conditions, instructions may be supplied digitally.
- **Control systems and software.** Requirements for protecting safety-related control systems against tampering and unintended influence are tightened.
- **Directly applicable.** The regulation applies in the same way in every EU country, without national variations.

## When do you need to look at this?

- You build your own machine or have a special-purpose machine built.
- You modify or modernise an existing machine, for example with new controls or new safety equipment. Read more about [retrofit](/en/services/retrofit/).
- You link several machines into a line or add a robot. Read more about [robotics](/en/services/robotics/).
- You lack documentation for a machine that is already in operation.

## How we work

1. **Review.** We look at the machine, how it is used and what documentation exists.
2. **Risk assessment.** We map the risks and the measures that are needed.
3. **Measures.** We carry out technical modifications ourselves, from guarding and safety components to control systems.
4. **Documentation.** Technical documentation and declaration of conformity.

We work on new machines, modified machines and machines built by others.
