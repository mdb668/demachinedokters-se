A smart change only has real value when everyone can work with it. That is why De Machinedokters make sure your team understands and masters the new systems. We provide support during and after implementation, so the transition runs smoothly and the improvements last. That way knowledge on the shop floor grows too.

We give hands-on training for operators, technicians and engineers, in which we explain how the new processes and improvements work. They learn how to prevent faults, carry out maintenance more smartly and work with the changes.

Alongside this guidance we also offer stand-alone training in lean manufacturing, process optimisation and error prevention, with which teams can develop their knowledge and skills further.
