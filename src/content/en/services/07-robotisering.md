Staff are scarce and repetitive work is heavy, dull and error-prone. Exactly the kind of work a robot does better. We automate operations such as **palletising and stacking**, **erecting and sealing boxes**, **packing**, **handling between machines** and parts of the **assembly**, and fit them into the line you already have.

We are at our best in the special cases: where a standard solution does not fit, where new techniques are needed or where real creativity is called for. Not the cheapest, but the most fun.

## Turnkey cobot palletiser for boxes

For stacking boxes we supply a complete cobot palletiser, **turnkey and TÜV-certified**.

![Cobot palletiser with a full pallet of boxes.](/images/robotisering/cobot-palletiseerder-dozen.jpg)

- **Standard pick point.** We install it, the boxes arrive at a fixed pick position and it can run straight away.
- Stacks up to **220 cm** high, on **two pallet positions**: the robot keeps stacking while the full pallet is taken away.
- Set a new stacking pattern on the touchscreen, **without programming**.
- **9 to 20 boxes per minute** and boxes up to around **38 kg**, depending on the version.

Price for turnkey installation with a standard pick point: from about **SEK 460,000** excl. VAT. Extras such as a roller conveyor, a double gripper or a modified infeed are additional.

Delivery time for the turnkey palletiser: within **about two months**. [Request one](/en/contact/).

## Robot or cobot? The biggest misconception

A cobot looks like the safe choice: it is slower and safe to touch. Out of the box, that is true. But what is in that cobot's hand, and what is it picking up?

Fit it with a knife, or have it handle a sharp or heavy product, and that cobot is no longer safe. Under the European machine safety rules you always assess the whole application, not just the robot arm. **In practice, a guarded industrial robot is therefore often faster, cheaper and simpler.** Stacking boxes is the exception: there a cobot really is the fastest and most economical choice.

## Brand-independent

We choose the robot that suits the application. We have extensive experience with **FANUC, ABB, KUKA and Doosan**. We buy the robot arm through the dealer or through our network for **refurbished robots**, which can make a considerable difference to the price. We do everything around it ourselves.

## How a robot project runs

1. **Defining what is needed.** Which products, how many per hour, what variation, what weights, and what has to keep working in the line?
2. **Simulation and concept.** We simulate the cell in advance, so reach, cycle time and collisions are known before anything is built.
3. **Prototype and build.** We design and build grippers, tooling, controls and safety ourselves, including the [CE marking](/en/services/ce-marking-machine-safety/) of the robot cell.
4. **Handover and implementation.** Fitting it into your line, commissioning it and training your operators and maintenance team.
5. **Service.** Because we also do maintenance and fault analysis, we stay involved with the robot cell after handover.

Duration of a robot project, from first conversation to implementation: **about six months**.

## What we do not do

We leave large robotic welding lines to others. We have no experience with them, and we would rather say so up front.
