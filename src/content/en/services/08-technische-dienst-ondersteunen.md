Your maintenance team is busy. There is always a new fault, so the cause of the previous one is left untouched. A cable tie here, an experienced operator who gets the machine running again there. That is how temporary fixes become the normal way of working.

## What we do

Together with your maintenance team we tackle the stubborn faults. We look for the real cause, independent of brand and across mechanics, electrics and controls, and solve it. Your people work alongside us. That way they learn how we find the cause, and can do it themselves afterwards.

## How a programme works

1. **Set the goal.** Together we decide which faults we solve and what your maintenance team should be able to do itself afterwards.
2. **Agreements on paper.** Goal, approach, who comes and how we report are put in writing beforehand.
3. **Get to work.** We determine the approach and direct our own specialists. We work on site, together with your maintenance team.
4. **Hand over.** What we solve, we document and teach to your people. You assess the result.

## How long does it take?

We know from experience that at least around 300 hours of working together is needed to see real results, for example three days a week for three months. Most clients extend after that.
