challenge: "Drilling holes in exactly the right places in a metal tube or bar takes precision and time. When it is done by hand, quality depends on who is working the line. At the same time, assembling different product variants is exactly the kind of work people are good at.\n\nSo the work had to be split so that the machine and the operator each do what they are good at."
approach: "We designed and built the ST-01 as a new machine around this split of work:\n\n- The operator places the plastic parts in the product holders and then puts the metal tube or bar in.\n- The machine presses everything into the right place and drills the holes where they are needed.\n- The machine then presents the drilled positions one by one to the operator, who sets a blind rivet in each.\n\nFor a different product, the operator swaps the product holders using two screws and selects a different recipe in the control system. There is room in the program to add more products later. We built the machine from standard, readily available components with long-term support, so parts will still be obtainable for years to come. We carried out the CE conformity assessment and CE marking ourselves."
