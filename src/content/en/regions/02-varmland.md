Do you have a machine that keeps playing up, a line that does not reach its capacity or an ageing control system for which spare parts are no longer available? De Machinedokters work on site in your factory in Värmland, together with operators, technicians and the maintenance team.

## What we help with

- **Downtime and recurring faults.** We find the root cause instead of patching things up. Read more about [fault analysis](/en/services/maintenance-fault-analysis/).
- **Ageing machines.** New controls and modern components in a machine that is still mechanically sound. Read more about [retrofit](/en/services/retrofit/).
- **Automation.** From a simple automation step to a complete robot cell. Read more about [robotics](/en/services/robotics/).
- **CE marking and machine safety.** Risk assessment and documentation ahead of the Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, which applies from 20 January 2027. Read more about [CE marking](/en/services/ce-marking-machine-safety/).

## Your contact in Värmland

Freek is your contact. Get in touch with a description of the machine or the problem, preferably with a photo or video.

We are based in Grythyttan in Hällefors municipality, in the heart of Bergslagen, and visit customers throughout Värmland, including Karlstad, Arvika, Kristinehamn and Säffle.
