## What data we process

When you send the contact form we process the details you provide: name, company, email address, phone number and your message. We use them to reply to your enquiry.

The form is sent through the Web3Forms service, which forwards the message to us by email.

Legal basis: we process the data on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry and, if it leads to an assignment, to perform the contract.

Retention: enquiries that do not lead to an assignment are kept for no more than two years. Data that forms part of our accounts is kept for seven years, as required by the Swedish Bookkeeping Act.

Processors: GitHub (hosting), Web3Forms (contact form) and Microsoft 365 (email).

## Cookies and statistics

This website sets no cookies and uses no visitor statistics.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to request access to your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to request restriction of processing, to object to processing and to receive your data (data portability).

## Contact and complaints

Contact us if you have questions about how we process your personal data: info@maskindoktorerna.se

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish supervisory authority, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), [imy.se](https://www.imy.se).
